# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors who want to read the currently displayed billboard, watch its tenure, join the live chatter, or pay to replace it with their own message.

## Product Purpose

The Last Billboard turns one shared web surface into a scarce public object: one live poster can occupy the wall at a time, and each takeover becomes part of its history.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, confrontational, and playful. The interface should feel like ink, paper, paste, and a public hoarding rather than conventional application chrome.

## Anti-references

Avoid polished SaaS dashboards, rounded component-library styling, decorative gradients, glass effects, generic icons, soft shadows, and motion that does not represent a physical takeover.

## Design Principles

- Treat the wall as a single physical object, not a collection of app cards.
- Keep the current poster unmistakably dominant.
- Make payment and takeover actions direct, legible, and materially styled.
- Preserve the strict print-shop palette and two-typeface identity.
- Use motion only when it communicates the physical act of replacing a poster.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion alternatives for the takeover sequence.
